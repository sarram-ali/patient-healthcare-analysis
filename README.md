# 📌 Project Overview

This Healthcare Patient Analysis project examines patient's record to uncover patterns in patient demographics, disease severity, treatment costs, healthcare utilization, doctor performance, and hospital efficiency. The analysis aims to help healthcare organizations improve resource allocation, manage treatment costs, and enhance patient care outcomes through data-driven insights.

# 🚨 Business Problems Addressed

Healthcare organizations often struggle with:

- Rising medicine costs with no clear cost control strategy
- Unidentified high-cost patient segments
- Poor visibility into repeat visit patterns
- Imbalanced doctor and hospital workloads
- No structured approach to chronic disease management

🎯 **Goal:** Use data analysis to improve operational efficiency, reduce avoidable costs, and enhance patient outcomes.

# 📈 Key Insights

## 🔹 Revenue & Cost Signals
- Total medicine expenditure across all patients: **$9.22 Million**
- **Chronic-Mild** and **Chronic-Moderate** segments contributed the largest medicine spend among the top 10% high-cost patients
- Patients with **Severe** conditions consistently incurred higher medicine costs than Mild and Moderate cases

## 🔹 Patient Demographics
- Dataset covers **1,000 patients** aged 18 to 79 (average age: 47.4)
- **Male patients (660)** outnumber Female patients (340)
- Most common blood groups: **A− and AB+** (200 patients each)
- Top insurance provider: **PrimeHealth** (300 patients, 30%)

## 🔹 Disease & Severity Distribution
- **Asthma** emerged as the most frequently diagnosed condition
- **Chronic diseases** account for the highest patient volume (396 patients)
- **Mild severity** is the most common across all disease categories (539 patients)

## 🔹 Treatment & Visit Patterns
- Average patient visits ranged from **5 to 7 annually** for Chronic and Cardiac conditions
- **Chronic disease patients** showed a **100% repeat visit rate** regardless of severity
- High-repeat-visit patients (>8 visits) were dominated by:
  - Diabetes
  - Hypertension
  - Arthritis
  - Coronary Artery Disease

## 🔹 Doctor & Hospital Performance
- **Cardiology** handled the most patients (50 unique patients, avg 6.5 visits)
- **Endocrinology** had the highest average visits per patient (7.1)
- **City Care Hospital** and **Lotus Hospital** recorded the highest repeat visit rates (>92%)
- **Greenfield Medical Centre** had the highest patient load among all facilities (49 patients)

# 💡 Business Recommendations

✔ **Strengthen Chronic Disease Management** — Invest in preventive care, digital follow-ups, and patient education to reduce long-term costs  
✔ **Target High-Cost Patient Segments** — Implement medication optimization and personalized treatment plans for Chronic-Mild and Chronic-Moderate patients  
✔ **Improve Resource Allocation** — Expand staffing in high-demand specialties: Endocrinology, Rheumatology, and Cardiology  
✔ **Boost Patient Retention Programs** — Use automated reminders and telemedicine for chronic condition follow-ups  
✔ **Develop Targeted Programs for Frequent Visitors** — Create specialized disease management programs for patients with 8+ annual visits

# 📂 Dataset Overview

| Column | Description |

| `patientid` | Unique patient identifier |

| `gender` | Male / Female |
| `age` | Patient age (18–79) |
